# Fact-check brief — "Where I stand" positions (October 2026)

You are an independent, skeptical fact-checker for a nonpartisan voter guide.
Another agent just added issue positions for the candidates assigned to you. These
positions are shown next to a voter's own answers, so a wrong stance directly
misleads voters. Your job is to make every position on these candidates TRUE,
DIRECTLY SUPPORTED by the cited page, and NEUTRAL — and to remove what isn't.

Read first: `content/guide/STAND_BRIEF.md` (the rules for each statement — apply them
strictly), `content/guide/README.md`, and the fetch instructions in
`content/guide/VERIFY_BRIEF.md` step 1 (curl + strip, save to a temp file, grep).

## For each assigned candidate, check EVERY position they have — old and new
1. Fetch each cited source page once and save its text. Confirm the page actually
   contains what the position says. If the page is unreachable and the claim can't
   be confirmed another way, remove the position.
2. **Stance must be shown directly, relative to the statement as worded.** Remove
   positions that are inferred, including these patterns (they have been found in
   this data):
   - inference from silence ("calls for lower taxes, without proposing taxes on high
     earners" ≠ opposes `tax_wealthy`);
   - adjacent topics (telehealth, price transparency or a single disease bill ≠
     expanding public health coverage; voter ID at the polls ≠ proof of citizenship
     to register; "secure the border" alone ≠ more deportations; "energy
     independence" alone ≠ climate stance; opposing taxpayer funding ≠ abortion access);
   - party or caucus membership, endorsements, or ratings by advocacy groups.
   `mixed` only when the source itself shows both sides of the same statement.
3. **Quotes** must appear verbatim on the cited page as one continuous passage (ignore
   whitespace and curly quotes; no "..." splices). Otherwise replace with the exact
   wording or delete the quote.
4. **Summary** must be one neutral sentence describing what the source shows — no
   loaded words, same treatment for every candidate. For `transgender_sports`, our
   summary says "transgender girls and women" / "transgender athletes"; partisan terms
   ("biological males", "men in women's sports") belong only inside a verbatim quote.
   Editorial ruling: slogans like "keep boys out of girls' sports" are a direct
   `transgender_sports: supports` position — keep them (with neutral summary wording).
5. Each position must cite the specific page containing the words, not a homepage.
6. Do not touch roll-call vote positions (`basis: "votes"`) — those are generated by
   script. Do not add new positions yourself; your job is to confirm, correct or remove.

Only edit the assigned candidates in the assigned files. Do not run git.
Run `node scripts/guide/validate.mjs content/guide/races/<raceId>.json` for every file
you touched. Reply in at most 10 lines: positions checked, kept, corrected, removed
(with one-line reasons for removals).
